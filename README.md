# NEyes

AI-Powered Diabetic Retinopathy Early Detection & Screening System.

## Features
- **NEyes AI Screening Engine**: Preliminary assessment of fundus retinal scans into 5 severity stages.
- **Quality Verification**: Image focus and illumination diagnostic check.
- **Grad-CAM Visualizer**: Explainable AI attention heatmap overlay for clinician reference.
- **Doctor Review Panel**: Clinician verification, modification, and report signoff.
- **Patient History & Screening Passport**: Longitudinal patient history tracking and digital health credential card.
